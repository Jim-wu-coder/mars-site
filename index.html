The problem

AI triage is easy to build and hard to trust.

Security teams want a model to take the first pass. Four things get in the way.

Too much to read

Reported mail and XDR incidents arrive faster than a small team can open them.

Confident is not correct

A model can sound certain and be wrong. A wrong "safe" is expensive.

The input is hostile

The mail was written by an attacker, sometimes with text aimed at the AI.

The data is sensitive

Reported mail holds internal correspondence. It cannot go to just any cloud service.

問題

AI 分流做起來容易，要讓人信任很難。

資安團隊希望讓模型先看第一輪，但有四件事擋在前面。

量太大

回報郵件和 XDR 事件進來的速度，超過小團隊打得開的速度。

有把握不等於正確

模型可以講得很肯定卻是錯的。一次錯誤的「安全」代價很高。

輸入帶有敵意

郵件是攻擊者寫的，有時還夾帶針對 AI 的文字。

資料很敏感

回報郵件裡有內部往來，不能送到任意的雲端服務。
